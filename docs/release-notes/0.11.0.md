# Babarify Media Player 0.11.0 (public beta)

Babarify Media Player is now a local-only player: it plays the music and videos on your PC and makes no
network connection of its own. This beta makes it smaller, sound better and easier to find your way around:
a new left bar, eight colour themes, genres, one search box, a mini player, and much quicker large libraries.
It is free and runs on Windows 10 (1809) and 11, 64-bit. Licensed under the GNU GPL v3 or later.

## Install or update

Run `BabarifyMediaPlayer-0.11.0-setup.exe`. It installs for your Windows user only, with no administrator
rights, into `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Babarify Media Player`, and updates 0.10.0 in place. Your settings,
library, history, favourites and playlists are kept. Close the player first.

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it doesn't recognise the app. Choose
*More info → Run anyway* only if the file's SHA-256 matches the published `SHA256SUMS.txt`:

    Get-FileHash .\BabarifyMediaPlayer-0.11.0-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256

The first time it runs, the library reads your songs once more in the background, for their genres. To go
back to 0.10.0, run its installer: it replaces the program, and 0.10.0 starts with an empty library because it
can't read the newer one. Your data stays on disk for when you update again.

## Local only, and smaller

- **No network:** network streams and internet radio are gone, and the engine no longer includes its network
  parts. The program is about 255 MB smaller, with a third fewer files, and starts in about 3 seconds the
  first time (it took 9).
- **AI subtitles and spoken-word search are removed.** If you used them, Settings → Privacy → *Delete old AI
  data* removes the speech models and subtitles they left. Nothing is deleted unless you choose it.

## Finding your way

- **A new left bar:** Home, Music, Videos, Playlists, Playing and Settings.
- **Music** has Songs, Albums, Artists and **Genres** (read from your songs' tags) and For you.
- **Videos** shows all your videos, Continue watching, Recently watched or Favourites.
- **One search** on Home finds songs and videos by title, artist, album, genre or file name.
- **Playlists:** drag songs to put them in order.
- **Mini player** (View → Mini player, or Ctrl+Shift+M), and **Always on top** in the View menu.
- **Eight colour themes:** Ocean Blue, Forest Green, Royal Purple, Amber Gold, Teal Breeze, Rose Pink,
  Classic Dark and Light, in Settings or View → Colour theme. Sunset is retired; if you used it, you get Amber
  Gold.

## Sound and playback

- **Volume boost without distortion:** above 100 %, the extra loudness now comes before a limiter, and 200 %
  means twice as loud.
- **The Room and Wider stereo effects** now really play (they never did in 0.10.0).
- **The equalizer** no longer makes everything quieter when you switch it on.
- **Moved or renamed files** keep their history, resume point, favourite, edits, bookmarks, lyrics and
  playlist entries.
- **The queue** comes back, paused, the next time you open the app (while play history is on).
- **Next video:** optionally play the next video in the folder when one ends (Settings → Fullscreen).

## Large libraries

Tested with 20,000 files: the Music tabs open in under a fifth of a second, Home search answers in about
40 ms, and the window stays responsive while folders are scanned.

## Privacy

No account, no telemetry, no update check, and now no network features at all. Files are never changed; one is
deleted only when you choose *Delete from disk* and confirm. See `PRIVACY.txt` in the program folder.

Known issues: `docs/beta/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`. Problem reports: Help → Report a problem.
